Fire door checks, kept in order.

Shutline helps building managers and landlords record quarterly and annual fire door checks in England, door by door, and keep the evidence on their own device.

  • A step-by-step check for each door, based on gov.uk fire door guidance.
  • Photos with location data removed before they are saved.
  • A record of attempts to gain access to flat entrance doors.
  • Reminders when checks are due.
  • PDF reports with Shutline Pro.
  • No account and no server. Your records stay on your device.

Shutline does not replace a fire risk assessment or a specialist inspection. Legal responsibility stays with the responsible person.
