Frequently asked questions

Which checks does Shutline follow?

The check steps follow regulation 10 of the Fire Safety (England) Regulations 2022 and the gov.uk fire door guidance. Check whether they apply to your building. Shutline does not replace a fire risk assessment or a specialist inspection.

What if I can't get into a flat?

Record each attempt on the door's screen, with the date, method and a note. The records show the steps you took to gain access.

Where are my photos?

Inside Shutline, not in your gallery. Location data is removed before a photo is saved. Please avoid photographing people.

How do I back up and restore?

In Settings, choose Create encrypted backup and set a password of at least 8 characters. Save the .shutline file somewhere safe. To bring it back, choose Restore from backup. Restoring replaces the records on this device. Records are not in iCloud or Google device backups, so this file is how you move to a new phone. Creating a backup needs Pro; restoring is free.

I forgot my backup password.

The backup can't be opened without it. We don't have the password and can't recover it.

Why didn't I get a reminder?

Check that notifications are allowed for Shutline in your device settings and that a reminder time is set in Settings.

How do I cancel or restore my purchase?

Cancel a subscription in your device's subscription settings: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. On a new device, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same store account. This also restores Lifetime.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a building, door or check on its own screen, or everything in Settings → Delete all data. See the Privacy Policy.
