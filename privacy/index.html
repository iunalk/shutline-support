Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Short version: Shutline has no account and collects no data. Your buildings, doors, checks and photos are stored only on your device. We never see them.

1. Who we are

Shutline is developed by İsmet Ünal, an individual developer. Contact: ismet.unal@icloud.com.

2. Data we collect

None. Shutline has no account or sign-in and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The app does not send your records to us or to any server. Its only network connection is to the App Store or Google Play to handle purchases. Links to these pages and to legislation and guidance open in your browser, outside the app.

3. Data stored on your device

Building names and addresses, door locations, check answers, access attempts, notes, photos, company details and settings are stored only in the app's own storage. Shutline's database and photos are excluded from iCloud device backups on iPhone and from Android cloud backup, so they do not leave the device that way.

4. Residents and other people

Your records can relate to buildings where people live, and some details may identify a person, such as a flat location. Shutline has no resident name field, and we ask you not to put people's names in notes or faces in photos. Under UK data protection law, you decide why and how these records are kept, so you are the controller for them and are responsible for handling them lawfully, including when you share a report or a resident notice. We have no access to them and do not act as your processor.

5. Camera and photos

Shutline asks for camera access only so you can photograph a fire door, and uses your photo library only when you choose a picture. A copy is saved inside the app, not in your gallery. Before it is saved, EXIF metadata such as GPS location and device details is removed. Your original photo is not changed.

6. Reports and backups

PDF reports, resident notices and .shutline backup files are created on your device. They leave it only when you choose to share, print or save them, and they go only where you send them. A backup is encrypted with a password you choose. We do not have that password and cannot recover it; if you forget it, the backup can't be opened.

7. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through any server of ours.

8. Purchases

Shutline Pro is sold and processed by Apple or Google. We never receive your name, payment card or billing details. The app only receives confirmation from the store of whether you have an active subscription or the Lifetime purchase.

9. How long records are kept

Records stay on your device until you delete them. You decide how long to keep them and can set a retention reminder in Settings. You can delete a building, door or check individually, or everything in Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. Your purchase is not affected.

10. Children

Shutline is a professional tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

11. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You still have rights under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, and under other laws that apply to you. We do not sell or share personal information. To ask a question, email ismet.unal@icloud.com. In the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Shutline is not affiliated with or endorsed by the UK Government.
